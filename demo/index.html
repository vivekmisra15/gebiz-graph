The model selects. People define.

A replay of the GeBIZ knowledge graph router. Singapore government procurement awards (FY2021 to FY2025) sit in a graph with human-written categories and metrics. The model only picks among them, every pick is logged with its probability, and below a confidence line it abstains. This page replays one real run; it makes no live calls.

How much of the awarded value could be classified?

Tenders with a category probability of 0.7 or higher count as classified; the rest are UNCLASSIFIED. Large contracts were classified more confidently than small ones, so coverage by value runs higher than by count.

By value (S$)By count (tenders)
Across all five years: 92.8% by value (S$114.9B of S$123.8B) and 87.2% by count (10,504 of 12,052 tenders). Awarded value is contract value at the award date, not cash spent.
Data: public GeBIZ awards from data.gov.sg (Ministry of Finance, Open Data Licence). Model: Jev (jev-1.13.0) by TypeSafe AI, used only to choose from closed lists. Router run on 5 October 2026 against the project's Neo4j graph; figures are replayed from its audit log, router threshold 0.7. A demo set of 12 questions, not an evaluation. Source and write-up are in the repository.
